Assume a gap-preserving compression procedure (a term of `MIPRE.GapCompression`) and an efficient universal machine. Then there is a computable map $\mathcal{M} \mapsto g(\mathcal{M})$ from Turing machines to synchronous game descriptions such that the game of $g(\mathcal{M})$ has synchronous value $1$ when $\mathcal{M}$ halts on the empty input and synchronous value at most $\tfrac12$ when it does not. Blueprint `thm:halting`, the conditional form of Theorem 12.2 of [JNVWY20]; the Lean statement quantifies over the compression and the universal machine.
