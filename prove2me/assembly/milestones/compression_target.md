The type `MIPRE.GapCompression` is inhabited: there is a polynomial-time procedure that maps a verifier $\mathcal{V}$ and a parameter $\lambda$ to a verifier whose game at index $n$ has a value-$1$ PCC strategy when $\mathcal{V}_{2^n}$ does, and value at most $\tfrac12$ when $\mathcal{V}_{2^n}$ has value at most $\tfrac12$. In the repository it is built from the three stage contracts by `GapCompression.ofPipeline` (blueprint `thm:compression`); on the platform the construction is the definition bundle of `Foundations/Pipeline/Compress.lean`, and the three contracts are the open leaves below.
