## Rejected or downgraded, with evidence

- **`traceStepProg` / `traceStep_bounded` (`BinaryTrace.lean:114,123` vs `BinaryTraceBits.lean:124,137`) and `MulState` / `mulStep` / `mulStepProg` (`BinaryPolynomial.lean:231–237` vs `BinaryQuotientPolynomial.lean:212–254`)**: name collisions, not duplicates. `BinaryTrace.traceStep` acts on `PowerState = BitStr × BitStr × BitStr` with unit stride; `BinaryTraceBits.traceStep` on `TraceState = Unary × BitStr × BitStr × BitStr` with a unary stride and calls `frobeniusBits`. `BinaryPolynomial.mulStep` folds over `Bool` (scalar Horner step); `BinaryQuotientPolynomial.mulStep` folds over `BitStr` and builds `addCoeffLists (bs.map (mulReduce p a)) …` (product of polynomials with quotient coefficients). No shared owner is possible.
- **`BinaryTrace.lean` (stride 1) as a special case of `BinaryTraceBits.lean` (stride `r`)**: mathematically `frobeniusTrace p a u = (frobeniusBits u p a, traceBits u (unary 1) p a)`, so the 165-line file could be redirected. Downgraded: both sides are tagged and guarded (`frobeniusTraceProg`, `evalBits_frobeniusTrace`, `evalBits_frobeniusTrace_trace`; `traceBitsProg`, `evalBits_traceBits`), and the consumer `MIPRE/Foundations/SAT/FieldTrace.lean:30–73` sits on `BinaryPower` while `BinaryTraceBits` sits on `BinaryQuotientOrbit → BinaryQuotientFrobenius → BinaryKernel → BinaryMatrixSolve …`, so the redirect drags the linear-algebra closure into the field-trace consumer. A tag redirect with import growth: low priority.
- **`Shoup.lean:47–59` (`shoupIrreducible_monic` / `_irreducible` / `_natDegree`, projections of `irreducibleBits_correct`) and `exists_shoup_irreducible`**: pass-throughs by design. `planning/shoup-axiom-removal.md` records "Preserve the existing public constructor and correctness interfaces" and "proving the existential contract from that witness"; keep.
- **`MIPRE.CL.proj` API (`Register.lean:37–101`) as a shadow of `Set.indicator` / `Finset.piecewise`**: the shape matches (`proj_proj` = `Set.indicator_indicator`, `proj_add_proj_compl` = `Set.indicator_self_add_compl`, …), but `Register.lean:25` states the file exists to fix vocabulary and simp lemmas, `proj` is used in 170+ files, and a replacement is a rewrite with no deletion. Rejected.
- **`mem_range_iff_pow_card` (`BinaryFiniteFieldDegree.lean:15`)**: searched Mathlib `FieldTheory/Finite/` by shape (`range ↔ … ^ Nat.card`); nothing. The closest is `Polynomial.splits_X_pow_nat_card_sub_X`, which the proof already uses. Keep.
- **`primitiveRoot_of_aeval_dvd_cyclotomic` (`BinaryPrimePowerLift.lean:29`)**: a short derivation from Mathlib's `isRoot_cyclotomic_iff` (`Mathlib/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Roots.lean:98`), one consumer; not worth a row.
- **`SchwartzZippel.lean`**: already built on `Mathlib.Algebra.MvPolynomial.SchwartzZippel`; `card_agree_le_of_natDegree` (`:145`) is `Polynomial.card_le_degree_of_subset_roots` plus a degree bound. No shadow.
