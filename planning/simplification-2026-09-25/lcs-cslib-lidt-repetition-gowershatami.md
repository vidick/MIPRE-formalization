# Simplification survey: `MIPRE/LCS/`, `MIPRE/Cslib/`, `Background/GowersHatami/`, non-vendored `Background/LIDT/`, non-vendored `Background/Repetition/`

Read-only survey, 2026-09-25. Method: module docstrings first; then the mechanical refcount
(`scratchpad/refcounts.json`) narrowed to declarations referenced at most once; then an
in-file and cross-file reachability fixpoint (`scratchpad/reach.py`, `scratchpad/lcsfix.py`)
whose roots are (a) any token match in a non-vendored Lean file outside the file/tree,
(b) blueprint `\lean{}` tags (multi-line aware), (c) guard files, (d) instances and
attribute-carrying declarations; every candidate below was then confirmed by reading the
call sites with `rg -F` on the final name component (`scratchpad/cnt.sh`, control:
`rescaleEquiv` → 2 hits, both its own file). Four `lean_run_code` probes were used.

One methodological finding that matters for the whole survey: **the mechanical dead scan
counts blueprint prose as a consumer.** A single `\texttt{MIPRE.LCS.SolutionGroup.
solutionGroupRepresentationOfEPRLoss}` in the prose of `def:solution-group`
(`blueprint/src/content/02_foundations.tex:375`) — not a `\lean{}` tag, not in
`blueprint_tags.txt` — is the only thing keeping about 1,400 lines of `MIPRE/LCS/` out of the
dead lists (candidate 1).
