## Rejected with evidence

- **`Dom` (Repeat/Dom.lean) vs `PDom` (Pipeline/PowDom.lean)** — nine parallel combinators and a dead bridge `PDom.ofDom` (PowDom.lean:121). Rejected: `PDom.le_final` yields a coefficient `(c(W+1)^m)^(K+1)` depending on `K`, while `Repetition.lean`'s complexity clause (`repSampler_timeBound`, `repDecider_timeBound`) needs `c, m, e` independent of `K`; PowDom.lean's docstring records exactly this. `PDom.ofDom` alone is deletable (dead list).
- **`MIPRE.Halting.lambda_bound` (Halting/LambdaBound.lean:100, ~60 lines)** — no Lean consumer (`PolyBounded.absorb`/`absorb_log` use only `log_lt_div`; Reduction.lean names it in a docstring). Tagged (`lem:lambda-bound`) and guarded: a bypassed paper-route lemma kept for the blueprint, not a deletion. Worth a blueprint comment that the Lean route absorbs through `PolyBounded.absorb`.
- **The `_of` → `_of_answerReduction` → unconditional ladder** (Halting/Corollaries.lean, Background/Pipeline.lean:50-89, MainTheorem.lean) for six theorems: all 18 rungs are blueprint-tagged; the middle rung could be inlined now that `MIPRE.gapCompression` exists, but that is a redirect-the-tag change, low priority. Likewise `halting_undecidable`/`halting_re` (Corollaries.lean:35,39) are one-line Mathlib pass-throughs but tagged.
- **`REPred.of_primrecRel_exists` (ValueApprox/RE.lean:34) vs `REPred.of_computable_exists` (Halting/Semidecider.lean:98)** — the computable one needs `[Inhabited β]`, so it is not a strict generalization, and the primrec one is upstream. No net deletion without restating one.
- **`Cost.primrec_natLeB`, `Cost.primrec_optionDataEqB` (Semidecider.lean:124,130)** — `Primrec.nat_le`/`Primrec.eq` are `PrimrecRel`, which hide the `Decidable` instance; the Bool-valued forms are needed to compose with `Computable`. Genuine wrappers.
- **`RawInt.PInt`/`GInt` (ValueApprox/RawInt.lean)** — checked: no file under Mathlib/Computability/ mentions `ℤ`; nothing to shadow.
- **`MIPRE.Halting.Obligations` (Reduction.lean:97)** — three fields, one producer (`CompressorSpec.toObligations`), one consumer (`halting_reduction`); tagged, and the consumer-first design is recorded in `planning/h4-assembly.md`. Not proposed.
- **`ProgD.dV/dNil/dC/dE/dL/dK` (Wrapper.lean:494-499) vs `pK/pC/pL/pE` (Compressor.lean:345-351)** — Data-level vs Prog-level constructors of encoded nodes; different types, both live. The six `d*_eq` rfl lemmas (Wrapper.lean:501-507) are dead documentation (dead list).
- **`Pipeline.readUnary` (UnaryArith.lean:24) vs `Introspection` `readUnary` (Foundations/Introspection/SourceCompilerParams.lean:82)** — different definitions (`map (const ())` vs `length`), different areas; noted only.
